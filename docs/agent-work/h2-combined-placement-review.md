## Review conclusion

The run-010 placement and offline evidence are internally consistent. I independently recomputed the original image, combined image, IPS, source, object, runner, manifest, and report hashes. The IPS decodes into the three declared non-overlapping records and reproduces the combined image byte-for-byte; the combined image differs from the original in 3,176 bytes, all within the declared Calc entry, Draw body, and `.text` page-tail ranges.

The split Draw body returns before the retained suffix, the two old main-to-suffix branch sources are in the replacement, and no external direct branch or aligned absolute pointer enters the retained Draw suffix under the audited scans. The final-address Draw, Calc sequence, and initializer caller results and negative controls match the manifest/report claims. I recommend the Draw ledger row be reclassified `non_matching` to reflect the implemented bytes, with a semantic record that retains the limits below. This is not H2 runtime or whole-title acceptance.

## Retained suffix and relocation review

The retained suffix is `[0x002CB588,0x002CB75C)`. The manifest records its nine direct branch targets and zero aligned absolute pointer hits. The two incoming edges from old Draw main, `0x002CB0AC -> 0x002CB74C` and `0x002CB580 -> 0x002CB6D4`, are both inside the replacement write and removed. The other seven direct edges originate and terminate within the retained suffix. Its back-edge at `0x002CB748` targets `0x002CB0B0` in the replacement body. Thus the replacement does not fall through into the retained suffix. This remains a static direct-branch/aligned-pointer conclusion; computed or indirect entries are outside the proof.
