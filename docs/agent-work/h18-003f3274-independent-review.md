# Independent review: initializer 0x003f3274

The original ARM entry and four-word literal pool form a 128-byte range ending before the next entry at 0x003f32f4. The candidate reconstructs the 15 global word values, while the full 800-byte ledger span remains outside this claim. The runner executes original and compiled candidate at the original address and checks watched final RAM, exact touched byte sets, callee-saved state, SP, continuation and CPSR; allowed stack scratch is deliberately excluded. I reran 32 cases with seed 739 and observed both all cases passing and five mutant controls detected. The first two worker seeds and their handoff are separately recorded.

This is evidence for this initializer under valid writable mapped RAM, not its real caller/registration path, fault behavior, concurrent observation or whole-game execution. The 120-byte compiled candidate fits the 128-byte entry-plus-literal region as an isolated blob in this experiment, but the production assembly wrapper contains additional entries: replacing or relocating it safely and testing the actual integrated image remain separate work. No production source, ledger or runtime claim is made.
