## Role and Behavior
- **Role:** Iterates equipped items / passive skill IDs to calculate and apply additive and percentage modifiers to character stats (HP, MP, Strength, Dexterity, Magic, Agility, Luck).
- **Details:**
  - Initialises HP scale and MP scale multipliers to 100%, and additive stat accumulators (St, Dx, Ma, Ag, Lu) to 0.
  - Queries item records via `camp_func_002840b8(item_id)`.
  - Filters records matching category `(raw >> 6) == 10`.
  - Adds percentage modifiers `b24 * 10` to HP scale and `b25 * 10` to MP scale.
  - Accumulates byte offsets 0x28..0x2C into 16-bit additive bonuses for St, Dx, Ma, Ag, Lu.
  - Scales HP and MP by their respective multipliers with division by 100, guaranteeing at least +1 bonus if scale > 100%, and clamps results to [1, 9999].
  - Adds stat bonuses to St, Dx, Ma, Ag, Lu and clamps each to [1, 999].
- **Differential Coverage:** Verified in Unicorn across empty item list, multiple bonus items, category filtering, fractional round-up preservation, HP/MP cap 9999, and stat cap 999 against original ARM bytes.
