## Unresolved / limits

- All ten callees are stubbed deterministically; their real contracts and side
  effects are not reconstructed here: `func_001311d8`, `func_0037f8e0`
  (virtual-dispatch thunk), `func_00235a4c`, `func_00235a08`, `func_00379ea0`,
  `func_00379e28`, `func_00379e08`, `func_0037fd70`, `func_002c615c`
  (`__aeabi_uidiv`-like unsigned divide, used as a lerp), `func_002a2224`.
- The original `smlal r3, r2, r1, r2` has `RdHi == Rm == r2`, which the ARM
  architecture marks **UNPREDICTABLE**. The replacement reproduces the
  behaviour observed in Unicorn/QEMU (and the documented intent: high word of
  the signed 64-bit product by `0x81020409`, plus the overlapped base); real
  3DS silicon is not guaranteed to produce the same high word for that
  instruction, so this single intermediate value carries a hardware-behaviour
  risk.
- Float-to-int conversion at step 8 relies on the ARM saturating
  `vcvt.s32.f32`; inputs that overflow the signed range are not specifically
  modelled but ordinary `static_cast<s32>` compiles to the same instruction.
- The identity/role of the `0x004119F8` template table and of the node/owner
  structures beyond the recovered offsets is not inferred.
- No emulator or in-game runtime scenario was executed. Evidence is the offline
  Unicorn differential plus mutation controls; whole-game and caller
  composition remain unverified.
