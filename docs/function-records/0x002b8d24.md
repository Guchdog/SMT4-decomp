## Limits

Evidence covers valid, aligned, non-overflowing pointers in mapped ordinary RAM. It does not cover arbitrary list/object overlap, invalid-address fault parity, MMIO, concurrent mutation, every manager-table pattern, or the positive-record nested service path through `0x002BDAC0`. Selector-12 alias coverage is one deliberate five-subobject topology. The two original caller compositions exercise selector 11; the direct caller scan is static and does not prove runtime-only dispatch. No whole-game equivalence is claimed.
