  a two-buffer IPC-style request (`func_002b5d70`), wraps the returned reply

- **Status:** Semantically equivalent C++ (`non_matching`). The compiled body is
  460 bytes versus the 448-byte original; the ledger row is classified
  `non_matching` with this record.

## Contract and Behavior

### Signature and ABI
```c
extern "C" u32 engine_func_002adc74(u32* out, const u32* descriptor,
                                    u32 arg3, u32 arg4,
                                    u32 arg5, u32 arg6, u32 arg7);
```
- `out = r0`, `descriptor = r1`, `arg3 = r2`, `arg4 = r3`; `arg5`, `arg6`,
  `arg7` are the three stack words at `[sp]`, `[sp+4]`, `[sp+8]` at entry
  (`0x002adc8c..0x002adc90`).
- Returns `u32`. Success returns 0; every failure returns the negative `s32`
  status from the failing call, or the literal `0xD8604659` for the
  allocation/lookup failures. On failure `*out` is not written.
- Callee-saved `r4-r11` are preserved; there is no VFP use. The frame is
  `push {r0-r3,r4-r11,lr}` (`0x34`) plus `sub sp,#0x74` = `0xA8`; the epilogue
  is `add sp,#0x84` plus a nine-register `pop` (`0x24`).
- Only the sign bit of the two status values is observed
  (`lsrs r0,r0,#0x1f` / `and r6,#0x80000000`).

### Memory and register facts
- `0x0044E650` is the absolute base of the shared block; the handle is the
  `u32` at `+0x10` (`0x0044E660`), loaded once at `0x002add0c`.
- `0x004D4358` is the callback pool object passed unchanged to
  `engine_func_002b5d0c`.
- `0x00420998` is written as the wrapper vtable; the failure path reads its
  `+0x2C` slot. The record vtable is whatever `func_0014636c` installed; the
  failure paths read its `+0x30` slot.
- The descriptor is read as four words: `+0x00`, `+0x04`, the low byte of
  `+0x08`, and `+0x0C` (`ldm fp,{r1,r3}` + `ldr r0,[fp,#8]` + `ldr r1,[fp,#0xc]`).
- Nothing outside the frame, the wrapper (`+0`/`+4`), the record (through the
  zero/constructor helpers) and `*out` is written.

## Limits
- All six direct callees are modelled by deterministic stubs; their internal
  semantics (the zero fill, the record constructor, the service request
  builder/`svc #0x32`, the pool allocator and the init routine) were not
  reconstructed here.
- The two virtual release targets (`record-vtable+0x30` and
  `wrapper-vtable+0x2C`) are runtime data and are installed by the test.
- The raw `svc #0x23` service is not reconstructed; only its position, its
  `r0` argument and its fall-through are preserved.
- The selector's `0xFFFFFF00` fill is the value the pinned `func_0014636c`
  leaves in `r12`. If that helper is ever replaced by C++ that does not leave
  the same scratch value, the original binary's behavior changes while this
  reconstruction stays fixed. The constant matches the pinned build.
- Fixtures are synthetic: the descriptor, shared block, pool, record, wrapper,
  vtable pages and all register inputs are scripted. No real service, thread,
  allocator or engine context is exercised.
- The C++ does not reproduce the original's exact frame, register allocation or
  stack-slot layout.
- No emulator, caller-composition or whole-game equivalence is claimed.
