reachable sentinel. It does not establish behavior for null/unreadable handles,
malformed or sentinelless tables, concurrent mutation, MMIO aliasing, or
arithmetic outside the tested signed-index domain. Caller compositions stub the
other services called by `0x00286928`; they do not show runtime selector
distribution. The two text-code callees remain original ARM, and their
user-facing mapping meanings remain unresolved. The two other direct callers
